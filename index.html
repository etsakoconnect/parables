Etsako Central LGA

Headquarters: Fugar

Celebrated for scenic landscapes, rich cultural traditions, and deep-rooted communal values.

Etsako East LGA

Headquarters: Agenebode

Nestled along the banks of the River Niger, renowned for vibrant riverine farming, fishing, and commerce.

History of Etsako

Telling exactly when, where and how it all started—that evolutionary process that has brought about the Etsako people as they are today—remains a journey into rich oral traditions. At the time of its earliest unfolding, there were no written records, and as such, there is no single authoritative source that can point to the 'factum realisimum' (most authentic fact) of the origins of the Etsako people.
